# used by services/youtube.py when YouTube's own captions are blocked or missing (Gemini watches the video)
Transcribe everything that is said in this video, in the language it is spoken, word for word. Do not summarise, translate or add anything.
Return ONLY JSON: {"language": "en|ur|hi|ps|...", "segments": [{"start": "m:ss", "text": "what is said from this time, about 10-20 seconds of speech"}]}
Rules:
- Timestamps must match when the words are spoken, in time order.
- Leave out music, sound effects and silence. If nobody speaks, return {"language": "", "segments": []}.
